Frequently asked questions

How do I open the meal box?

Choose a meal and a box style on the cover screen and tap Order. Then physically unfold your phone: the lid lifts open and the meal appears on the inner display.

Can I play on a regular (non-foldable) iPhone?

Yes. On a regular iPhone, simply tap the closed box to open it, and use the Close Box button when you're done.

How do I eat the meal?

Tap the food to take one bite at a time. Keep tapping until the box is empty, then fold your phone shut (or tap Close Box) and swipe the empty box away.

What's free and what's in the Starter Pack?

Fried Chicken & Fries with the Classic Foam Box is free to play. The Starter Pack is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks Smash Burger Meal, Grilled Salmon Rice, the Brown Paper Box and the Meal Prep Box.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Choose Meal screen and tap a locked Popular meal or box style to open the Starter Pack sheet, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

I bought the Starter Pack but it's still locked.

Check your internet connection, then try Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't unlock, fully close and reopen the app. If the problem continues, email us with the date of purchase and we'll help.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

When are more cuisine packs coming?

More cuisines are on the way in future updates. Want a specific dish? Email us and tell us what you'd like to eat next!

Is this a real food delivery service?

No. Unfold Meal is a virtual interactive toy and entertainment app. No real food is ordered or delivered.
