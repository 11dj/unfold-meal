How it works

Your phone's hinge is the lid of the box.

1

Pick your meal & box

Browse meals and box styles on the cover screen, then tap Order.

2

Unfold to open

Open your phone and watch the lid lift off to reveal a full meal on the wide inner display.

3

Tap to eat

Tap the food to take one bite at a time until only a few crumbs are left.

4

Fold, close & toss

Fold your phone shut to close the empty box, then swipe it away and pick your next craving.
