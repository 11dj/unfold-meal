Privacy Policy

Short version: Unfold Meal does not collect your personal data.

Effective date: October 26, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Unfold Meal ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. We designed the App so that it works entirely on your device and does not collect, store, track, or share any personal information.

2. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account details
  • Location data
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or contacts data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking data

The App does not require registration or login, and it does not contain third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

3. Data stored on your device

The App may save small settings on your device, such as your selected meal and box style or whether you've completed your first meal. This information stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you delete the App.

4. In-app purchases

Optional in-app purchases (such as the Starter Pack and future Cuisine Packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or have access to your payment details, billing information, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of your information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Information from Apple

If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as crash reports or download counts). This information does not identify you personally.

6. Children's privacy

Because the App does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

7. Changes to this policy

If future versions of the App add features that handle personal data, we will update this Privacy Policy and the effective date above before those features are released.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email kittisq@gmail.com.
